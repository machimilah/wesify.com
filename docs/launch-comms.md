# Launch communications (§66)

Draft copy for the founders to adapt. Rules: no claims we can't back up (no user counts, accuracy numbers or partnerships we don't have), and no money language, because W$ is play money.

## One sentence

> Wesify is a free prediction game where you call how many likes a video will get in 24 hours, with play money.

## Short founder explanation

> Every day, some videos explode and most don't. We built Wesify to make calling it a game: each market asks how many likes a video will have 24 hours after it opens, prices move as the likes come in, and you can buy the range you believe in or sell early. It's play money, so it's about being right, not about paying to play. We're opening a small beta and want people who live on their For You page.

We only use public info, link back to your video (we never re-upload), and you can have any video removed with one message. We're inviting a few creators into our program: connect your account, open a market on a new upload, and see how your audience calls it. Creators can't trade on their own videos. Interested in a 15-minute call?
